WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

Effective September 26, 2026

Website visits

Fonts and images are served with this website. GitHub Pages hosts the public pages and may process technical request information to deliver and secure them. We do not use advertising trackers on these pages. Where your browser permits it, a source and approved campaign label stays within the current tab so the firm can understand which link brought an inquiry. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable those labels. We do not retain raw referring addresses, advertising click identifiers or other query details for this purpose.

Matter inquiries

The inquiry form collects your name, email, service, property county and type, party names, and any broker or deadline you choose to provide. It also records the page and campaign source where available. Cloudflare processes and stores these submissions for the firm. Its Turnstile service helps protect the form from automated abuse. The firm uses the information for inquiry, conflict and engagement review and to respond to you.

Please submit only the requested basic details. Do not upload or send confidential documents, identification, payment instructions or sensitive facts before the firm agrees to receive them. An inquiry does not establish an attorney-client relationship.

Security and access

Inquiry records are kept in a private queue available to authorized firm systems. The site does not publish your submission. Abuse controls use short-lived keyed hashes rather than storing raw IP addresses in the inquiry database. Hosting and security providers may separately process technical information under their policies. No internet service can promise absolute security.

Email, disclosure and retention

Email you send to the firm is handled through Google Workspace. Records may be processed by the service providers supporting our communications and operations, and disclosed where required by law or professional obligations. The firm retains inquiry and conflict-review information as needed for those purposes and its recordkeeping obligations. It does not sell inquiry information.

Questions

Email nick.harris@crownhudsonlaw.com about information supplied to the firm, including a request to correct it. Client matters may be governed by additional engagement terms and professional duties.
