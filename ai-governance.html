Practice guidance from Nick Harris, New York attorney.
Updated September 15, 2026.

What is AI governance?

AI governance assigns responsibility for how an organization selects, uses and oversees AI. A usable program identifies permitted uses, accountable owners, required evidence, human review and the events that trigger reassessment. The practical question is who can approve a use and on what basis.

Is an AI risk framework the same as legal compliance?

No. NIST’s AI Risk Management Framework is voluntary guidance organized around Govern, Map, Measure and Manage. It can structure the work, but the applicable law, contract terms and business facts still need their own analysis. A completed framework checklist does not establish legal compliance.

When should an AI vendor be reviewed?

Review the identified use before committing to a provider or sharing sensitive information. Revisit the decision when data inputs, available actions, model capabilities or contract terms materially change. For example, approval to draft a response does not automatically authorize sending it to a customer.
