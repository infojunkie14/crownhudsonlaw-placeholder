Updated September 15, 2026.

What should an incident-readiness review cover?

Identify who receives an incident report, gathers facts, preserves evidence and makes escalation decisions. Review contacts for technical response, legal analysis, communications and business continuity. CISA’s small-business guidance recommends identifying these roles before a suspected incident.

Does every security event require breach notification?

Notification depends on the event, the information involved, affected people, applicable jurisdictions and contractual duties. A security alert alone does not answer those questions. An incident plan should assign responsibility for timely legal review and preserve a record of the facts supporting each notification decision.

Does a vendor’s security report settle the legal review?

No single report answers every contract or deployment question. Check its scope, period, exceptions and relevant service, then compare that evidence with the supplier’s promises and your own obligations. Identify what is still missing and who will resolve it before relying on the assurance.
