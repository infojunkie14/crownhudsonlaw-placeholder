Security governance

Review policy commitments, oversight and evidence supporting the security decisions your business is making.

02

Vendor & contract review

Examine security assurances, incident-notice duties, allocation of responsibilities and the limits of supplier evidence.

03

Incident readiness

Clarify escalation, preservation and notification decision processes before an incident. Develop an agreed scope for tabletop or readiness work.

WHAT AN ENGAGEMENT CAN DELIVER

  • A review of the agreed security policies, terms and responsibilities
  • Identified gaps in evidence, escalation and contractual obligations
  • Written recommendations for readiness and follow-up review

Deliverables depend on the scope agreed before work begins.
